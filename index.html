Questions, answered.

Is this a native app?

expand_more
Ciroo runs directly on your device, saving to your home screen and feeling exactly like a native app without the hassle of app store approvals or updates.

How long does it take to get my app?

expand_more
Once we finalize your categories and branding assets, your custom app is typically ready to install on your devices shortly after.

Can my employees see total revenue?

expand_more
No. Employee accounts are restricted to 'entry only' by default. Only designated admin accounts (like the owner) can view historical records, dashboards, and total revenue figures.
